Record first-pass valid specs / total, final valid handoffs / total, onboarding minutes per member, invented or missed agreements, false drift findings, correctly attributed seeded findings, mismatches found before versus during integration, and every rescue. Attach redacted evidence locally. A pilot passes only when the complete loop finishes without maintainer rescue and unresolved findings are agreed, repaired or explicitly accepted. Otherwise keep its original failure and rerun after the concrete issue is corrected.
